**Conventions**
- **[SQUARE BRACKETS]:** facts that the company or counsel must supply. Do not fill them from assumptions.
- **CONFIRM:** a product statement taken from the plan or the research. Check it against the build actually deployed.
- **OI-n:** an open item, listed in section 8 of `dpia-outline.md`.
